COVER: Your content deserves a permanent home

SLIDE 1: That Instagram post you spent hours on? It's buried within 48 hours. But a blog post on your website can bring you traffic for years. While you sleep. While you're on holiday.

SLIDE 2: Your website is the only place online you truly own. No algorithm changes. No platform policy updates. No one can take it away from you.

SLIDE 3: But most website templates treat blogs like an afterthought. They look stunning in the demo — then fall apart when you add real content with real images.

SLIDE 4: What to look for: clean reading experience, mobile-first design, fast loading speed, easy navigation between posts, and built-in email capture. Non-negotiables.

SLIDE 5: The biggest mistake? Picking a template because the demo looks pretty. That demo has 3 perfect posts. Your blog has 47 posts with varying image quality. Always test with your real content first.

SLIDE 6: The right template doesn't just display your posts. It guides readers toward a next step — subscribing, reading more, booking a call. Not a dead end.
