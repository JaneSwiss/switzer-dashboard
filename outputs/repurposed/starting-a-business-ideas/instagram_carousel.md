COVER: Your business idea isn't on a trending list

SLIDE 1: Most "business ideas" lists are useless. They throw 50 options at you without helping you figure out which one fits your skills, your budget, or your actual life.

SLIDE 2: The businesses that work share four things: they solve a specific problem for a specific person, they can start small, they don't need huge upfront investment, and they use skills you already have.

SLIDE 3: Ask yourself this: what do people already come to you for help with? That answer is probably closer to a real business than anything on a trending list.

SLIDE 4: "Life coach" is too broad. "Career coach for women returning to work after maternity leave" is a business. Specificity is what makes people choose you over everyone else.

SLIDE 5: Service businesses you can start with just a laptop: coaching, virtual assistance, social media management, freelance writing or design, online tutoring. No inventory needed.

SLIDE 6: The best business idea is the one you can actually execute. Not the one that sounds impressive. Start with what you know. Build from there.
