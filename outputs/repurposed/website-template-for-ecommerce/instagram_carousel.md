COVER: Pretty templates don't sell.

SLIDE 1: You picked a template because it looked amazing in the demo. Added your products. Hit publish. And now you're wondering why nobody's buying.

SLIDE 2: Here's the problem: a template that looks good isn't the same as one that converts. Structure matters more than aesthetics.

SLIDE 3: Check this first — does it actually work on mobile? Not just shrink, but reorganize for smaller screens. Most of your visitors are on their phones.

SLIDE 4: How fast does it load? If your site takes more than three seconds, your visitor is already gone. Speed isn't optional.

SLIDE 5: Can someone buy without friction? Six clicks and a password reset will tank your sales. Simple checkout wins every time.

SLIDE 6: Match the template to how your customer shops. One signature product needs storytelling space. Fifty products need strong filtering. Different problems, different structures.

CAPTION:
I see this constantly — someone grabs a gorgeous template, adds their products, and wonders why nothing's selling.

The template looked perfect in the demo. But demos don't show you if the structure actually fits how your customers need to shop.

Before you pick any ecommerce template, check for mobile-first design, fast load times, clear product pages, and a checkout that doesn't feel like work.

Pretty is nice. But functional is what pays you.
