SLIDE 1: The businesses that succeed aren't the ones with the most complicated setup. They're the ones that actually launch. Simple beats perfect every time.

SLIDE 2: Get specific about what you're selling and who it's for. "Busy coaches who need professional Instagram content" works. "People who like nice things" doesn't.

SLIDE 3: Stop comparing every platform. Etsy gives you built-in traffic. Your own website gives you control. Pick one and launch. Add the other later.

SLIDE 4: Your store needs to look professional enough that people trust you with their money. That's the bar. Not award-winning. Just trustworthy.

SLIDE 5: Start with one product or a small collection. You can expand later. Right now, your job is to prove the concept works — not build an empire overnight.

SLIDE 6: You don't need everything figured out on day one. Start simple. Refine as you go. The learning happens after you launch, not before.

CAPTION:
I spent years thinking I needed the perfect setup before I could sell anything online.

The truth? I started on Etsy with zero audience and figured it out as I went. 27,700+ sales later, I can tell you the complicated setup isn't what matters.

What matters is choosing one platform, getting clear on who you're selling to, and launching before you feel ready.
