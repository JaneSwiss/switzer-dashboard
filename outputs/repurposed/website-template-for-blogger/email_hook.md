What actually matters? Whether it loads fast, works on mobile, and makes it easy for visitors to do what you want them to do — subscribe, buy, or book a call.

That gorgeous template with all the fancy animations? It might be actively hurting your blog if it takes more than three seconds to load. Your readers will leave before they see how beautiful it is.
