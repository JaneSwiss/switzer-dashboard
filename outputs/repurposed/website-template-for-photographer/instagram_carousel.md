COVER: Your website is losing you clients.

SLIDE 1: You have beautiful photos. Your portfolio speaks for itself. But visitors land on your site and leave without booking. The problem isn't your work. It's your website.

SLIDE 2: Most photographer websites have the same issues: slow load times from full-resolution images, no clear path to contact, and mobile that looks like an afterthought.

SLIDE 3: Every extra click loses potential clients. If someone has to dig through menus to find your contact page, a percentage of them will give up before they get there.

SLIDE 4: Your website is not a gallery. It's a sales page with pictures. The job isn't to display your work beautifully — it's to guide visitors toward one action: getting in touch.

SLIDE 5: Before choosing a template based on looks, check these: Does it load fast? Can you reach the contact page in one click? Does it look intentional on mobile — not squished?

SLIDE 6: A beautiful template that makes booking difficult will always lose to an average template that makes booking obvious. Choose function first.

I've seen this pattern hundreds of times.

The photographer with mediocre images but a clear website books more clients than the one with stunning work buried in a confusing layout.
