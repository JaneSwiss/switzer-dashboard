COVER: Your Shopify setup order is backwards.

SLIDE 1: Most product sellers spend week one picking themes and installing apps. Then wonder why nothing works when they try to take their first order. The setup order matters more than the setup itself.

SLIDE 2: Handle foundation settings first. Payments, shipping, taxes — before you touch anything pretty. These affect every single transaction your store will ever process.

SLIDE 3: The plan math no one shows you. Basic charges 2.9% + 30¢ per transaction. The $105/month plan charges 2.6% + 30¢. You'd need $22,000/month in sales before upgrading pays for itself.

SLIDE 4: Buy your custom domain on day one. The free yourstore.myshopify.com makes you look like a hobby seller. $15-20/year for a proper domain is non-negotiable.

SLIDE 5: Set up Shopify Payments before adding products. You'll get funds in 2-3 business days with no extra fees. Third-party gateways add roughly 2% on top of their own rates.

SLIDE 6: Then add products. Then worry about your homepage banner color. This order saves you weeks of backtracking and fixing things that should have been right from the start.

CAPTION:
I've watched too many product sellers spend three weeks on their homepage while their shipping zones aren't even configured.

The Shopify setup process has a specific order that makes sense. Foundation first (payments, shipping, taxes), products second, pretty stuff last.
