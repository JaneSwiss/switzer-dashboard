COVER: Your templates are wasting your time

SLIDE 1: Most free Instagram templates look amazing in the preview. Then you add your logo and the whole thing falls apart. Colours clash. Fonts fight. It looks like two brands collided.

SLIDE 2: A useful template does three things. It matches your brand. It's easy to edit. And it solves a specific content problem. Pretty alone isn't a strategy.

SLIDE 3: If you need a 20-minute YouTube tutorial to change the text, that template isn't saving you time. You should understand how it works in under two minutes.

SLIDE 4: "Pretty" doesn't help you post. You need templates for testimonials, services, education, or showcasing your work. A template without a clear purpose will sit unused forever.

SLIDE 5: Before you download anything, ask: can I swap in my brand colours easily? Does the font match my vibe? Will this look like my content — or just someone else's template?

SLIDE 6: Free templates are a great starting point. They help you understand what you like and get you posting. But the wrong ones cost you more time than they save.

CAPTION:
Spending 30 minutes fighting a template that almost works is 30 minutes you're not spending on clients.

Do that every week and it adds up to over 25 hours a year. More than three full working days — gone.

Free templates can absolutely work for your business. But only if they actually fit your brand, edit fast, and help you create specific types of content.
