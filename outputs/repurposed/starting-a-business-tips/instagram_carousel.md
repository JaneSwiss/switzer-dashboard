SLIDE 1: That feeling when you sit down to work on your business and end up with fifteen browser tabs open and nothing done? You're not behind. You're just following the wrong advice.

SLIDE 2: Most "starting a business tips" are written by people who haven't built anything recently. A 40-page business plan isn't progress. It's procrastination in a nice outfit.

SLIDE 3: Get specific about who you serve. Not "anyone who needs help." A real person with a real problem. The clearer you are, the faster the right people find you.

SLIDE 4: Professional branding isn't optional. You don't need to spend thousands — you need a clear logo, 2-4 colors, and consistency. When everything matches, you look established.

SLIDE 5: Your website matters more than your Instagram following. Social media is rented land. Your website is the one place you own — and it's where trust actually happens.

SLIDE 6: You don't need to figure it all out before you start. You need to start with the things that actually build trust and bring in clients. Everything else can wait.

CAPTION:
My first year in business, I designed for everyone. Wedding invitations, café menus, recipe cards — you name it.

By the end of that year, I had no clear brand and no one could refer me because they didn't know what I actually did.

The shift came when I stopped trying to serve everyone and got specific about who I was really helping.

That one change made my messaging make sense. Made the right people find me. Made selling feel easier.
