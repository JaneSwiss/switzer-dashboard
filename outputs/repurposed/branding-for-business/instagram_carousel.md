SLIDE 1: Your potential clients are making decisions about your business in seconds. They're scrolling your Instagram, landing on your website, clicking through your services — and deciding if you look legit or not.

SLIDE 2: That decision has almost nothing to do with your skills. It comes down to visual and verbal signals. If your colors clash, your fonts are inconsistent, and your logo looks rushed — you're losing people before they even engage.

SLIDE 3: Branding isn't just a logo. It's the full picture: colors, fonts, photography style, voice, and how all of it works together to create a feeling. Most people skip this and wonder why nothing converts.

SLIDE 4: Inconsistent branding creates friction. Visitors can't explain it, but something feels off. Maybe your website uses one palette and your Instagram uses another. These small disconnects erode trust fast.

SLIDE 5: Consistent branding does the opposite. It signals you know what you're doing. It makes your business feel established — even if you launched six months ago. It removes the mental work for potential clients.

SLIDE 6: You don't need 30 brand assets. You need five: a logo that works at any size, a color palette, two fonts, a photography style, and a consistent voice. Apply them everywhere. That's it.
