COVER: Your portfolio isn't converting. Here's why.

SLIDE 1: A beautiful template means nothing if visitors leave without taking action. Your portfolio is a sales tool, not a museum.

SLIDE 2: Most templates are designed to look good in a preview — not to get you clients. They prioritise flashy animations over clear navigation.

SLIDE 3: Before you pick anything, get clear on what your portfolio needs to do. Book calls? Sell products? Land freelance gigs? That answer changes everything.

SLIDE 4: Check the mobile preview first. Over 60% of visitors find you on their phone. If it looks clunky there, they bounce.

SLIDE 5: Fast and simple beats flashy and slow. If your template takes four seconds to load, you've already lost half your visitors.

SLIDE 6: Look for built-in contact forms and testimonial sections. If people can't easily reach out or see social proof, you're making this harder than it needs to be.

You have incredible work. But when someone asks for your website, you send them to Instagram or mumble something about "still working on it."

The problem is rarely the work itself. It's the presentation.

Most creatives pick templates based on how pretty the demo looks. But that demo is filled with professional photography and perfect copy you don't have yet.

What actually matters? Clear navigation. Mobile design. Fast loading. A path that guides someone from your homepage to your work to your contact page without confusion.
