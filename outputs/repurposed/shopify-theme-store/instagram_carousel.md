SLIDE 1: Most sellers choose themes by scrolling screenshots and picking something pretty. Three months later they're frustrated, paying for apps, and wondering why their store feels slow.

SLIDE 2: The catalog size rule: 1-30 products? Free themes are almost always enough. Shopify's free options now include features that used to be premium-only.

SLIDE 3: 30-150 products? Evaluate based on specific feature needs, not aesthetics. Ask "does this solve a problem I actually have?" — not "does this look nice?"

SLIDE 4: 150+ products or complex variants? Premium themes often earn their cost. Better filtering and variant handling reduces customer confusion and support questions.

SLIDE 5: Over 70% of ecommerce traffic is mobile. Yet most sellers fall in love with themes on desktop and only check mobile as an afterthought. This is backwards.

SLIDE 6: Before buying any theme: test it on your actual phone. Not a browser preview. Your real phone, with real scrolling, real tapping. That's what your customers experience.

CAPTION:
A theme isn't a vibe decision. It's a business decision.

The wrong choice creates problems you won't notice until you've already lost sales — slow load times, awkward product displays, apps you shouldn't need to buy.

The right choice gives you a foundation you can build on for years.
