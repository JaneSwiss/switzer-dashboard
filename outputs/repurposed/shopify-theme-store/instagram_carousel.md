COVER: Your theme choice is a business decision

SLIDE 1: Shopify's theme store has just 268 themes. Not thousands. That small number means your choice matters more than you think — for speed, conversions, and how professional your store actually looks.

SLIDE 2: Two themes (Dawn and Trademark) power roughly 25% of all Shopify stores. If you're using either one without heavy customisation, you look like one in six competitors. That's not branding — that's blending in.

SLIDE 3: Free themes in 2026 include features that were premium-only a few years ago. Flexible sections, product recommendations, reviews integration, quick-buy. For many sellers, free is genuinely enough.

SLIDE 4: Premium themes ($180-$350) make sense in specific situations: 200+ products needing advanced filtering, or brands that rely heavily on storytelling and editorial layouts. Otherwise? You're probably overpaying.

SLIDE 5: Most sellers browse by "popular" or "new," pick what looks nice in the demo, and realise months later their theme is slowing their mobile site or missing basic features. Demo pretty ≠ right for your business.

SLIDE 6: Before you commit: check mobile speed scores, confirm the features you actually need are built in (not app-dependent), and ask whether this theme fits your catalog size. Future you will thank you.

Your Shopify theme isn't just a design decision.

It affects how fast your store loads, whether visitors trust you enough to buy, and how much time you spend fighting with apps that don't play nicely together.
