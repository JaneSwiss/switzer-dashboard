COVER: Your store isn't ugly. It's leaking sales.

SLIDE 1: Most store owners blame their photos or their theme when sales don't come. But that's rarely the actual problem. The real issue is usually hiding in three specific places.

SLIDE 2: Leak #1: your product pages answer "what is this?" but not "why should I buy it now?" Every page needs to handle objections before they become reasons to close the tab.

SLIDE 3: Leak #2: shipping costs that surprise people at checkout. Nothing kills a sale faster than an unexpected $12 fee on a $25 product. Show it early or bake it in.

SLIDE 4: Leak #3: too many checkout steps on mobile. Every extra tap is a chance for someone to get distracted, second-guess, or just give up. Simplify ruthlessly.

SLIDE 5: The platform you pick matters less than you think. The transaction fee math matters way more. A "free" platform can cost you $200/month once you add everything you actually need.

SLIDE 6: Start with products listed and checkout working. Then shipping that makes sense. Then branding. That sequence sounds boring but it's the one that actually makes money.

CAPTION:
Beautiful product photos won't save a store that leaks sales at checkout.

I see this pattern constantly — someone launches with a gorgeous theme, gets traffic, and wonders why nobody's buying. The problem isn't what they think.

It's shipping surprises. Product pages that describe but don't sell. Checkout flows with too many steps on mobile. Platform fees they didn't calculate properly.

The fix isn't a redesign. It's knowing where the leaks are and plugging them in the right order.
