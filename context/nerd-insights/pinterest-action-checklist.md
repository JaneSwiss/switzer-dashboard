# Pinterest — Action Checklist for Switzertemplates

*The Nerd Agent's own suggestions for applying everything in the [Pinterest summary](pinterest-summary.md) to the business — proposals to evaluate and tick off, not facts from the sources.*

---

## Foundation

- [ ] Confirm the Pinterest account is set up as a free business account with discovery/search visibility turned on
- [ ] Build a keyword spreadsheet for each product line (3-in-1 bundles, Wix websites, branding kits, Instagram template packs) using the autocomplete method and the "alphabet soup" method (keyword + each letter A-Z)
- [ ] Run those keywords through Pinterest Trends to check search volume and spot seasonal peaks
- [ ] Create boards named after exact keyword phrases / official Pinterest interest categories — e.g. "Branding Kits for Female Entrepreneurs" rather than a generic "Switzertemplates" board

## Pin creation

- [ ] For each product, create three distinct pins — different photo, hook, and keyword angle each — instead of reusing one pin with a new title
- [ ] Mix problem-aware pins ("my Instagram feels inconsistent," "my website looks unprofessional") with solution-aware pins ("Wix website templates for coaches")
- [ ] Show the visual transformation the product creates — before/after brand makeovers, mockups of a finished website or Instagram feed — not just the product on its own
- [ ] Use real photos and real product/customer examples rather than AI-generated imagery — Pinterest's AI evaluates the actual image content
- [ ] Link every pin straight to the relevant product page (the $38 branding kit pin → that listing, not the shop homepage)

## Scheduling & cadence

- [ ] Stagger the three pins per product roughly a week apart over three weeks rather than posting them together
- [ ] Schedule seasonal or product-specific pins 45-60 days ahead of expected demand (e.g. new-year business launches, "refresh your brand" periods)
- [ ] Batch-create a month of pins at a time and schedule through Tailwind or Pinterest's own scheduler — both test as having no negative effect on reach

## Mindset & ongoing management

- [ ] Treat Pinterest as a roughly one-hour-a-week system rather than something that needs daily attention
- [ ] Run it as a 60-day test with clear traffic and conversion metrics before judging the results — sources note 30-60 days before traction shows
- [ ] If it proves itself, consider handing the weekly routine to a VA (sources cost this around $30-35/hour)
- [ ] Revisit keyword research and board strategy periodically — consistency was named as the single biggest factor separating accounts that work from ones that don't
